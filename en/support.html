Support

Found a bug, the game will not start, or you have an idea? Write to us — we read everything and reply within a few working days.

  • Getting in touch
  • What to send
  • Common questions

Getting in touch

Email: support@snowops.game. This is the only support channel. We never ask for passwords, codes or payment details: if someone asks for them in our name, it is not us.

What to send so we can help faster

  • Your device model and OS version: for example, Samsung Galaxy A54 on Android 15, or iPhone 13 on iOS 19.
  • The game version: it is shown in the game settings and on the store page.
  • What happens and what you expected instead: the more detail the better. A screenshot or a short video helps a great deal.
  • When it started: after a game update, after an OS update, or straight after installing.

We use your email only to reply to you; see the Privacy Policy for details.

Common questions

The game will not start, or crashes while the level loads

SnowOps builds the level on every launch, so it needs both time and memory. Close other apps, make sure at least 2 GB is free, and restart the phone. If that does not help, write to us with your device model: it may not be supported yet.

The game stutters

Lower the graphics quality in the game settings. On iPhone the game supports iOS 13 and later; on Android it runs best on devices no more than a few years old with 4 GB of memory or more. If the frame rate drops on a particular phone, send us the model: we collect this by hand, from emails, and tune the settings per device.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not to play: the game runs entirely offline. When a connection is available, it downloads updated models, sounds and translations from our site on its own — small packs, no account, and nothing about you is sent. There are no ads and no purchases.

Where is my progress stored, and how do I move it?

Progress is stored on the device only. It is included in the system backup if you have Google One or iCloud backup enabled, and is restored with it on a new phone. There is no separate sync between devices.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the game or clear its data in the system settings. Step-by-step instructions are on the Data deletion page.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you got the game from: Google Play, the App Store or RuStore. Contact their support; each store has its own deadlines and rules.

What languages is the game in?

The game is available in Russian and English. It follows your phone’s language on first launch, and you can switch it in the settings.
