In short

SnowOps works entirely offline. It does not collect personal data, does not store it on our servers and does not share it with third parties. The game has no sign-up, no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no internet access. Everything the game saves stays on your device and is removed together with the app.

Below we explain in detail what that means in practice, and what happens to the data handled by the app stores and by our website.

1. Scope of this policy

This policy describes the processing of data in connection with:

  • the SnowOps mobile game for Android (package identifier com.snowops.game) and iOS (identifier com.romaxa55.snowops), hereinafter the “Game”;
  • the snowops.game website, hereinafter the “Website”;
  • your support requests sent by email.

The data controller is the developer of the Game, hereinafter the “Developer” or “we”. Contact details are given in section 13.

2. What we do not collect

The Game does not collect and does not transmit to us or to anyone else:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details;
  • device identifiers or advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID, IDFA);
  • your location, precise or approximate;
  • the contents of your contacts, photos, files or clipboard;
  • camera or microphone recordings;
  • gameplay session statistics, behavioural analytics or crash reports sent by the Game itself;
  • payment details: the Game has no purchases.

The Game contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or social networks. The app does not request internet access permission and does not open any network connections.

3. Data on your device

In order to work, the Game saves the following in the app’s own protected storage:

  • your progress and saved games;
  • settings: volume, control sensitivity, graphics quality, button layout;
  • technical performance logs, which help the Game choose the right settings for your device.

These files contain no personal data, never leave your device and are not accessible to other apps. They are deleted when you uninstall the Game.

If backups are enabled in your operating system settings (Google One, iCloud), a copy of these files may end up in your backup. That is governed by your device settings and by Google’s or Apple’s policies, not by us.

4. Permissions

The Game does not request permission to access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files, phone, notifications or the internet. If an app store lists a permission in the description, it relates to standard operating system features (vibration, for example) and not to any collection of data.

5. App stores

You obtain the Game through Google Play, the App Store or RuStore. These services process data relating to your store account and to the installation and updating of the app under their own rules, which we do not control:

  • Google Privacy Policy (Google Play);
  • Apple Privacy Policy (App Store);
  • RuStore Privacy Policy.

From the stores we may receive only anonymised aggregate statistics: the number of installs, countries and device versions, together with crash reports if you have allowed them to be sent in your operating system settings. This data cannot identify you and is used to fix bugs and maintain compatibility.

6. Website

The snowops.game website is static: it has no forms, no user accounts, no visitor counters and no advertising or analytics scripts. We set no cookies and embed no third-party services: fonts, images and videos are served from our own server.

The website’s hosting provider may keep standard access logs (IP address, request time, page address, browser type) for attack protection and diagnostics. Such logs are kept for a limited period and are not used to identify visitors.

7. Support requests

If you write to our support address, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us in your message: your device model, system version, a description of the problem or screenshots, for example.

We use this data solely to reply to you and to fix the issue. We do not share it with third parties, do not use it for mailings and do not combine it with any other source. Correspondence is kept for no longer than 12 months after the matter is closed, and is then deleted. You may ask us to delete it sooner; see section 10.

The legal basis for this processing is your consent, given when you send us a request, and our legitimate interest in supporting our users. Processing is carried out in accordance with applicable data protection law, including Federal Law No. 152-FZ “On Personal Data” for users in Russia and the GDPR for users in the European Union.

8. Children

The Game is rated 16+ and is not intended for children under 13. Since the Game collects no personal data, we do not collect children’s data. If you believe that a child has sent us personal data through a support request, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to find out what data about you we process, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict its processing, to withdraw your consent and to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Since the Game collects no data, in practice these rights relate to your correspondence with support. To exercise any of them, write to the address in section 13. We respond within 30 days. There is no need to separately object to processing by the Game: simply uninstall the app.

10. Data deletion

All game data is stored on your device. To delete it, uninstall the Game or clear its data in your system settings. To have your support correspondence deleted, send a request from the same address you wrote from. Step-by-step instructions for Android and iOS are on the Data deletion page.

11. Changes to this policy

If the Game starts processing any new data — when online features are added, for example — we will update this policy in advance, change the date at the top of it and, where required, ask for your consent inside the Game. The current version is always available at snowops.game/privacy.html and via the link inside the Game.

12. Storage and transfers

We do not store data about players of the Game on any servers, so no cross-border transfers take place. Support correspondence is stored in the Developer’s email service, protected by a password and two-factor authentication.

13. Contact

For any questions about personal data, write to support@snowops.game with “Personal data” in the subject line. The party responsible for processing personal data is the developer of SnowOps.
