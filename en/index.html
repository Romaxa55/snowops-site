Get in quietly. Get out quietly.

SnowOps is a single-player campaign about an operative dropped beside a polar military base with one job: get inside, do the work and be gone before dawn.

There is no regenerating cover and no arrow pointing at the objective. There are binoculars, a map, patrols on a timetable and a lot of snow you stand out against. Like the shooters of the late nineties, on a phone screen.

  • A single-player campaign — no online, no strangers in your match
  • Plays offline: on a plane, on the underground, out of town
  • No ads, no purchases, no energy timers

What you get

We are making the game we would want to play on the road ourselves: slow, attentive, with no energy timers and no feeling that something is rushing you.

  • Patrols walk their routes

    Guards work the perimeter on a schedule, cameras sweep, searchlights hunt in the dark. Learn the rhythm, find the gap and slip through. Or raise the alarm and deal with what follows.

  • The base lives without you

    Freight trains run to a timetable, a truck pulls out of the garage, the watch changes by the clock. Any of it can be your cover, if you are in the right place at the right time.

  • Binoculars matter more than a rifle

    Open ground and long distances. Look first, plan second, shoot only if there is no way around it. There are not enough rounds for everyone.

  • Built for a phone

    Big buttons, a thumb-friendly layout, short sessions. The game plays offline and asks for no account and no needless permissions.
