Data deletion

SnowOps has no accounts and no servers: everything the game saves sits on your device. You can delete it yourself in a minute.

What data there is

The game stores your progress, your settings and technical performance logs on your device. They contain no personal data and are never sent to the developer. There is no in-game account, so there is nothing to delete. More details in the Privacy Policy.

Android

To delete only the data and keep the game:

  1. Open Settings → Apps → SnowOps.
  2. Select Storage.
  3. Tap Clear data (on some devices: Manage space → Delete all data).

To delete everything: press and hold the game icon on your home screen and choose Uninstall, or open Google Play or RuStore → the SnowOps page → Uninstall. All of the app’s data is removed together with the app.

iOS

On iOS the app’s data is deleted together with the app:

  1. Press and hold the SnowOps icon on the Home Screen.
  2. Choose Delete App → Delete.

The Offload App option under Settings → General → iPhone Storage keeps the data; to remove everything, choose Delete App.

Backups

If system backups are enabled, a copy of the game data may remain in Google One or iCloud until the next backup is made or until the backup is deleted. You can manage your backups in your Google or Apple account settings; the developer has no access to them.

Support correspondence

If you have written to our support address, we keep your correspondence for up to 12 months after the matter is resolved. To have it deleted sooner, send an email with the subject “Delete my data” to support@snowops.game from the same address you wrote from. We will delete the correspondence within 30 days and confirm this by email; once you have received the confirmation, that message is deleted too.
